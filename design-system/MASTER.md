# 福 bank design system

International student banking simulation. The visual direction is calm, distinctive and financial, with a prominent snapshot and clear paths to everyday tasks.

## Palette and typography

- Navy/petrol `#102b32` for the navigation and balance hero; white for hero foreground.
- Teal `#087f70` for actions, selection, focus and links; white action foreground.
- Warm white `#f7f8f5` background, white cards, `#e2e7e2` borders.
- Gold `#b88732` is a decorative accent. Dark brown text on warm cream for planning panels.
- Keep the existing bundled Inter variable font for reliable loading. Use 400 body, 500 labels, 600 headings and tabular financial figures. Hero value uses a fluid 32–56px scale; ordinary supporting text wraps.

## Structure

Financial snapshot first; three summary metrics; compact action shortcuts; multi-currency balances; spending, cash flow and commitments. Navigation groups are Everyday banking, Plan & manage, Your workspace. Keep every existing route and account query parameter.

Use a 4/8px spacing rhythm, 20px card radii and 24px feature-panel radii. Visual weight comes from contrast, scale and grouping. Do not invent historical charts, growth percentages, live feeds or security claims.

## Accessibility and motion

Keep semantic headings, labeled controls, visible focus, the skip link and mobile focus trap. Interactive targets should be at least 44px. Use 180ms color/border transitions. Honor reduced motion. Keep data and simulation disclosures visible and approval controls intact. Check 375px portrait, landscape, desktop and 200% text sizing.
