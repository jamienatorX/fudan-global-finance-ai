# FU Bank — Fufu, your financial agent

FU Bank is an AI banking-agent prototype that helps users understand and manage fictional finances through natural-language conversation and voice input. Its assistant, Fufu, connects requests to deterministic tools for account queries, spending analysis, subscription detection, card controls and investment simulations.

Users review exact changes before execution. The authenticated engine applies permission tiers, cumulative daily transfer checks, action-bound authenticator verification, owner isolation and durable audit records. Persisted workflows support interruption, conservative compensating actions and a secure team-operated support inbox.

A public browser simulation provides an accessible demonstration, while signed-in banking implements server-enforced controls. Recurring-payment results show confidence, supporting settled charges and cancellation capability. Supported read queries continue working without a language-model response. Authenticated fictional transfers support exact future schedules and birthday reservations. Real merchant payments remain unavailable and are never reported as completed.

The project combines model-assisted intent interpretation with validated calculations and allowlisted operations, keeping financial results and execution authority outside model-generated prose.
