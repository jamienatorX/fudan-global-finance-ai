Fufu on-device live dictation uses unmodified sherpa-onnx 1.12.20 WebAssembly engine and bilingual streaming Zipformer model.
Engine: https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/tree/v1.12.20 (Apache-2.0).
Model: https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/sherpa-onnx-streaming-zipformer-bilingual-zh-en-2023-02-20 (Apache-2.0 model card).
Original model: https://huggingface.co/pfluo/k2fsa-zipformer-chinese-english-mixed
Training code: https://github.com/k2-fsa/icefall/tree/master/egs/librispeech/ASR/pruned_transducer_stateless7_streaming
Official packaging recipe: https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/blob/v1.12.20/.github/workflows/wasm-simd-hf-space-zh-en-asr-zipformer.yaml
Release archive SHA256: e889207cd6a84973cbc028c05115cdd778ae8e277867b83169644be5b3a63c53
Model data is split into transport shards by FU Bank; reconstructed bytes are unchanged. See LICENSE.txt.
